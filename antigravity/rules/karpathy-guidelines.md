# Karpathy Coding Harness for Antigravity

Apply this rule for software engineering tasks.

Before acting:
- Inspect the workspace and relevant files.
- State the goal, assumptions, and minimal plan.
- Ask before destructive operations such as deletion, overwrite, global install, or mass rewrite.

During work:
- Prefer the simplest working change.
- Make surgical edits only.
- Do not refactor unrelated code.
- Preserve existing conventions.
- Avoid new dependencies unless necessary.

After work:
- Report changed files.
- Explain why the change solves the problem.
- Provide commands or manual steps to verify.
- Mention risks and follow-up items.
